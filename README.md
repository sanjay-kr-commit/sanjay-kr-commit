# About Me

Hi, I'm **Sanjay**, a CSE developer who likes computers, Linux, and building things.

I started using Linux in **2020**. At the time, I had an old machine with 4 GB of RAM, and Windows 10 was not a particularly pleasant experience on it. I started with Garuda Linux, then moved through Ubuntu and Pop!_OS before eventually settling on Arch Linux.

What began as a way to make an old computer more usable gradually turned into an interest in understanding how computers and operating systems work. I spent a lot of time customizing KDE Plasma, and eventually that curiosity moved toward the shell.

These days I spend a lot of time with **zsh, Neovim, terminals, Linux, and developer tooling**. I also enjoy split keyboards, especially the whole combination of mechanical keyboards and RGB lighting.

Outside of programming, I enjoy **anime, manga, books, novels, and music**. I also sing sometimes and have spent some time learning music.

I'm not particularly interested in trying to present myself as an expert at everything. I mostly like learning, experimenting, solving problems, and building things that I find useful or interesting.
